What Architetto Does For You

Architetto is your tripwire. Every night he visits your published pages, one by one, and checks that they're actually working — because an ad campaign pointing at a broken page burns money faster than any bad targeting ever could.

He is the cheapest insurance on the team: the whole job is noticing breakage on the day it happens rather than at the end of the month.

Everything Architetto Can DoNightly site crawl

Is the site actually up?

  • Crawls your published pages and posts every night
  • Raises a critical alert on any page returning an error — the ones your ads may be pointing at right now
  • Measures how long each page really takes to load, and flags the slow ones that quietly raise your cost per lead
  • Only judges pages that genuinely loaded — an error page proves nothing about your headings

Is your tracking alive?

  • Detects whether an analytics tag is actually present and firing on each page
  • Flags pages with no tracking at all — where conversions vanish silently

Trust and hygiene

  • Flags insecure content loading on a secure page, which browsers increasingly block outright
  • Flags duplicate page titles that make Google guess which page you meant

Doing double duty

  • Carries Spia's 53-point on-page audit on the same crawl, so your site is visited once rather than twice
  • If the SEO audit hits a problem, the health crawl still completes — the two are deliberately isolated

Where Architetto Sits In The Swarm

Straight Talk

Architetto watches your site — he does not build or edit it. He reads your pages and reports what's wrong; changing them is your call, or Penna's draft.

He needs a WordPress connection. On other platforms he has nothing to read, and he'll say so rather than reporting a clean bill of health.

The speed check is a real page-load measurement, not a full performance lab report.
