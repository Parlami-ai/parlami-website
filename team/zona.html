What Zona Does For You

Where you appear on Google Maps depends on where the person searching is standing. Zona scans your area as a grid — dozens of individual points across your neighbourhood — and shows you exactly where you appear and where you're invisible.

The valuable part isn't the map. It's that Zona tells you which of those dead zones you could actually win.

Everything Zona Can DoGrid map + 6 checks

The map

  • Scans a configurable grid across your area — a point every few streets, out to a radius you choose
  • Records your exact map position at every single point
  • Records who beat you at each point, with their review count and rating
  • Produces a pinned visual map with a clear legend, plus a plain sentence on how far your visibility reaches north, south, east and west

Winnable versus unwinnable

  • Separates dead zones caused by simple distance — which no amount of work will fix — from dead zones caused by a genuinely stronger rival
  • Tells you how many lost spots are actually closeable, and how many are physics
  • Names the rivals beating you, and flags which of them you could realistically overtake
  • Gives a single verdict for your area — invisible, dominant, distance-locked, out-gunned, or mixed — with a prioritised list of what to do

The checks

  • Flags when your overall top-three coverage across the area is too thin
  • Flags individual searches where your top-three coverage is weak
  • Flags searches with a cluster of points sitting at 4–10 — one push from the map pack
  • Flags large dead zones where you don't rank at all
  • Flags wildly inconsistent ranking across the area, which usually points at a listing problem

What Zona Plugs Into

Zona runs live Google Maps searches from each grid point through a search-data provider, using your verified Google business listing as the anchor.
