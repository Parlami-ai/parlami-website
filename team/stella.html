What Stella Does For You

Stella watches your reputation the way a good manager would — noticing the rating slipping before it becomes a pattern, and never letting a review sit unanswered long enough to look like you don't care.

She also does something less obvious and quietly valuable: she reads your reviews for the exact words your customers use, and passes them to the agents writing your ads.

Everything Stella Can Do7 nightly checks

Watching the rating

  • Checks your rating and review count daily
  • Flags when your average drops below a healthy floor
  • Flags a meaningful decline compared with the previous period, before it becomes a trend
  • Flags a stalled review pace — when new reviews slow to a trickle

Replies

  • Flags a backlog of unanswered reviews
  • Drafts a reply for each review, matched to its sentiment and using the reviewer's own first name
  • Only echoes a service word the reviewer themselves used — no invented specifics
  • Blocks any reply offering a discount, refund or incentive, which violates Google's rules
  • Blocks accusatory language on negative reviews, and strips the giveaway AI phrasing
  • Every draft waits for your approval — nothing is ever posted automatically

Getting more reviews

  • Recommends a review-request funnel when your rating or pace has headroom
  • Hosts a public feedback page: happy customers are sent to leave a public review, unhappy ones are routed to a private form that reaches you instead
  • Builds review-request messages with proper opt-out wording

Mining reviews for language

  • Extracts the practical words customers repeat, and passes them to Annunci as search-term candidates
  • Extracts the emotional language from your best reviews, and passes it to Faccia as ad-copy angles
  • Filters out your own brand terms so the signal stays clean
  • Does this with plain word analysis rather than a language model, so nothing is invented

Business profile posts

  • Drafts Google Business Profile posts across offer, story, product and event formats
  • Enforces real limits: length caps, no markdown, required justification, proper offer terms and dates
  • Reviews your monthly mix and warns when it drifts too far toward one post type

Where Stella Sits In The Swarm

Your agents don't sit in a chat room talking to each other — they share one workspace. Every night, on a fixed schedule, each agent writes what it found onto a shared findings board and into a single approval queue. Every other agent can read that board, and so can you. A few agents also hand each other specific intelligence directly. The result is one queue of decisions instead of fifteen inboxes.
