What Catena Does For You

Who links to your website, and whether your business details are consistent everywhere they appear, quietly decides how much Google trusts you. Catena audits both — and does it carefully, because getting link work wrong is worse than not doing it.

He is deliberately the most cautious agent on the team.

Everything Catena Can Do10 nightly checks

Your link profile

  • Summarises referring domains, total links and the follow/nofollow mix
  • Reports "unknown" rather than guessing when the underlying data can't tell — no confident falsehoods
  • Tracks new and lost referring domains between checks, and raises the alarm on a meaningful loss
  • Flags domains sending you an unusually high number of links

Risk

  • Flags domains scoring high on toxicity
  • Falls back to its own spam heuristics when no toxicity score exists — spam top-level domains, spam anchor text, sitewide link patterns
  • Flags over-optimised commercial anchor text, a classic penalty trigger
  • Flags a single commercial phrase repeated across many different domains
  • Insists a human reviews every flagged domain, because these heuristics can misfire on legitimate sites

Business listings

  • Checks your name, address and phone number for mismatches across listings
  • Flags missing tier-one listings — Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook and the BBB
  • Stays quiet when listing data simply hasn't been gathered yet, rather than claiming you're listed nowhere

Building presence, carefully

  • Works from a curated catalogue of directories, with packs for childcare, home services, legal, medical, restaurants, weddings and fitness
  • Hard-blocks link-scheme and spam directories before they ever reach you
  • Records directories you're already on rather than drafting a duplicate submission
  • Pre-fills submissions so every listing gets byte-identical business details
  • Deliberately paces itself to a small number of new submissions per run — a natural footprint, not a burst
  • Checks weekly whether new listings have actually gone live
  • Collects directory confirmation emails and surfaces the link for one human click — it never clicks them itself

Competitors and outreach

  • Lists the domains linking to a competitor but not to you
  • Identifies your most link-worthy pages from real search data
  • Drafts outreach emails with a specific reason for each target

Straight Talk

Catena flags risky domains for review — he does not generate or submit a disavow file. Disavowing is a serious, hard-to-reverse action and it stays a human decision.

He never fills in a directory form himself, and no outreach email has a way to send.

He needs a backlink data source connected; without one he works from imported data and says so.
