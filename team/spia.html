What Spia Does For You

Spia watches how people actually find you on Google — which searches you're winning, which you've started slipping on, and which you're one small push away from owning. Then she does something unusual: she hands what she learns to five other agents.

She is the swarm's research department. More of the team runs on Spia's intelligence than on any other agent's.

Everything Spia Can Do7 rules + 53 page checks

Your rankings

  • Flags searches where your position dropped meaningfully compared with the previous period
  • Flags high-volume searches sitting just off page one — the ones worth a push
  • Flags searches where you rank well but nobody clicks, which is a headline problem, not a ranking problem
  • Flags searches that are growing fast, so you can move while they're rising
  • Raises a critical alert when someone else outranks you for your own brand name
  • Flags keyword cannibalisation, where several of your pages compete for the same search and split the signal

Answer engines

  • Tracks referral traffic arriving from AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and others — as a visibility signal
  • Drafts an llms.txt file, the emerging standard for telling AI crawlers what your site is about

A 53-point page audit

  • Checks metadata, headings, content depth, structured data, media, internal links and URL structure
  • Checks the E-E-A-T signals Google uses to judge whether a page deserves trust
  • Checks accessibility basics
  • Checks how readable your pages are to AI systems
  • Rides along on the site crawl rather than hammering your server with a second one

Watching competitors

  • Discovers who is actually competing with you — combining who's advertising on Google, who's running Meta ads, who ranks organically, and who's physically nearby
  • Weekly competitive briefing: keyword gaps, a domain scoreboard, competitors you aren't watching yet, and their live ads
  • Flags competitor ads that have been running long enough to be proven winners
  • Flags new entrants to your market and shifts in a competitor's messaging angle
  • Flags competitor claims that carry compliance risk

Turning findings into work

  • Turns the top findings into assigned action packets with an owner, waiting in your approval queue
  • Scores whether its past recommendations actually moved rankings
  • Runs on a hard weekly spend cap for paid data, and drops back to free data rather than quietly overspending

Where Spia Sits In The Swarm

Your agents don't sit in a chat room talking to each other — they share one workspace. Every night, on a fixed schedule, each agent writes what it found onto a shared findings board and into a single approval queue. Every other agent can read that board, and so can you. A few agents also hand each other specific intelligence directly. The result is one queue of decisions instead of fifteen inboxes.
