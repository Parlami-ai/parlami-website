What Regista Does For You

Video ads live or die in the first three seconds. Regista studies the video ads actually working in your market, pulls out what makes their openings work, and tells you precisely when your own video has run its course.

His most useful trick is a discipline: he will not report a hook he can't prove was said.

Everything Regista Can Do5 checks + hook intelligence

Learning what hooks work

  • Finds competitor video ads running in your market
  • Transcribes them and distils what makes the opening work
  • Discards any lesson whose quote doesn't literally appear in the transcript — no invented insight
  • Records the structure, the opening seconds, the actual duration and the call to action

Knowing when to refresh

  • Flags video fatigue — long-running video whose click rate has collapsed
  • Reads Meta's retention data to tell you *which* part failed
  • If people don't get past the opening, the brief calls for a rebuilt first three seconds
  • If they drop off in the middle, the brief calls for a tighter edit instead

Briefing the next cut

  • Writes the hook, the voiceover lines and a shot-by-shot storyboard
  • Produces it in three shapes — vertical, square and widescreen
  • Works to a short-form default length
  • Runs the same sector compliance checks as the rest of the creative studio

Watching your video library

  • Raises a critical alert on video running without approval
  • Flags video with no captions — most social video is watched on mute
  • Flags video longer than its placement allows
  • Flags video longer than short-form guidance

Learning from your own video

  • Compares your captioned versus uncaptioned video performance
  • Compares your short versus long video performance
  • Reports nothing rather than inventing a pattern when there isn't enough video to judge
