What Musa Does For You

Musa answers the question every advertiser eventually asks: what should the next ad actually say? She studies the ads your competitors have been running longest — because an ad running for months is an ad that's working — and drafts fresh concepts in your voice.

She is careful about it. Everything she drafts cites the evidence behind it, and nothing is copied.

Everything Musa Can Do5 checks + creative studio

Learning from what's proven

  • Studies competitor ads in your market and identifies the ones running long enough to be proven winners
  • Shows each cited ad alongside the draft, with how long it's been running, so you can check her reasoning
  • Leads with your own best-performing ad as the benchmark to beat, with its real leads, click rate and cost

Drafting, with guardrails

  • Writes headline, body copy, call to action and art direction for each concept
  • Automatically discards any draft sharing a long verbatim run with a competitor's copy
  • Automatically discards any draft that names a competitor
  • Caps its own confidence — these are starting points for your judgement, not verdicts

Turning a concept into an image

  • Renders a draft as a finished, correctly-sized ad image on request
  • Produces a three-format bundle — square, portrait and vertical — for full placement coverage
  • Runs compliance checks tuned to your sector before anything renders
  • Refuses to generate entirely if your creative rules don't permit it, rather than guessing
  • A creative bundle cannot even be submitted for approval until all three formats have actually rendered

Watching your creative library

  • Raises a critical alert on any asset running that hasn't been approved
  • Flags images below minimum resolution
  • Flags images at the wrong shape for where they're running
  • Flags creative fatigue — long-running assets whose click rate has fallen through the floor
  • Flags placements with no approved asset covering them

Learning from your own results

  • Identifies your winning creatives — the ones beating your account average click rate
  • Works out your real creative refresh cadence from how fast your ads actually fatigue

Where Musa Sits In The Swarm

Your agents don't sit in a chat room talking to each other — they share one workspace. Every night, on a fixed schedule, each agent writes what it found onto a shared findings board and into a single approval queue. Every other agent can read that board, and so can you. A few agents also hand each other specific intelligence directly. The result is one queue of decisions instead of fifteen inboxes.
