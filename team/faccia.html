What Faccia Does For You

Faccia watches your Facebook and Instagram advertising every single day. He knows the difference between an ad that's genuinely failing and one that's still learning — and that distinction alone saves more money than most optimizations.

He is the agent most likely to tell you to do nothing yet. That's a feature.

Everything Faccia Can Do19 nightly rules

Money going nowhere

  • Flags ad sets whose cost per lead has drifted above your baseline
  • Flags campaigns and ad sets that spent real money in the last week with nothing to show
  • Flags ad sets that were converting last week and have gone quiet this week
  • Flags campaigns underspending their own daily budget — usually a delivery problem, not a demand problem

Creative burnout

  • Catches creative fatigue: the same people seeing your ad too often while click-through quietly collapses
  • Catches audience saturation, where frequency climbs and cost per lead worsens together
  • Flags rising, expensive audience prices before they eat the month
  • Flags ads with enough delivery to judge and weak click response
  • Flags ad sets running a single ad with no creative test in flight

What Meta itself is telling you

  • Reads Meta's own quality, engagement and conversion rankings to say *which part* of an ad is failing — the image, the offer, or the landing page
  • Reads the real learning-phase status straight from Meta rather than guessing at it
  • Flags ad sets whose budget mathematically cannot exit the learning phase at their current cost per lead

Video

  • Flags video ads whose first three seconds fail to hold anyone
  • Flags video that loses viewers partway through
  • Routes weak-hook video to a rebuilt-opening brief, and weak-retention video to a tighter-edit brief

Waste hiding in the breakdown

  • Placement waste — the placement quietly taking a big share of spend while badly underperforming the rest
  • Day-of-week gaps where one weekday is consistently worse
  • Daily anomaly detection on spend, leads, clicks, impressions and cost per lead

Account guardrails

  • Alerts if your ad account isn't active, or is near its spend cap
  • Alerts on rejected ads
  • Alerts when there's no pixel, or the pixel has gone stale
  • Alerts before your access token expires and the data silently stops
  • Alerts when lead permissions are missing
  • Spots organic posts performing well enough to be worth promoting

Instant lead capture

  • Pulls new Meta lead-form leads every hour, around the clock
  • Records each new lead and starts its follow-up clock

Verdicts, not just problems

  • Classifies every ad as winner, fatigued, underperformer, testing, zombie or inactive — with a clear leave / scale / rotate / pause call
  • Publishes positive verdicts too, so you know what's working and shouldn't be touched
  • Measures whether its own past recommendations actually improved things
  • Batches changes that would reset Meta's learning, so approving five fixes doesn't cost you five learning resets

Where Faccia Sits In The Swarm

Your agents don't sit in a chat room talking to each other — they share one workspace. Every night, on a fixed schedule, each agent writes what it found onto a shared findings board and into a single approval queue. Every other agent can read that board, and so can you. A few agents also hand each other specific intelligence directly. The result is one queue of decisions instead of fifteen inboxes.
