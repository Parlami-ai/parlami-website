What Piazza Does For You

Piazza keeps your social presence honest. She notices when you've gone quiet, tells you which posts actually earned attention rather than just existing, and drafts the next round in your voice — shaped to each platform's real limits.

Every post waits for your yes. She is built so that approving a post and publishing it are two separate, deliberate acts.

Everything Piazza Can Do5 checks + social studio

Drafting posts

  • Writes drafts for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X from a topic you give her
  • Applies each platform's real limits — Instagram gets more room and more hashtags, X gets far less
  • Writes from your approved brand brief, so the voice is yours rather than generic
  • Generates a matching square image for a post on request
  • Discards anything that looks like it contains sensitive or credential-like content
  • If the drafting service is unavailable, nothing is saved — no half-written posts left lying around

Keeping you honest

  • Flags an active account with nothing scheduled
  • Flags how long it's been since you last posted on each platform
  • Flags platforms whose engagement rate has dropped below a healthy floor
  • Flags when too many of your posts are going out with no image
  • Flags a standout post worth amplifying

A serious approval gate

  • Approving a post fingerprints the exact wording and the exact image you approved
  • Approval covers that image for that channel specifically — not everywhere, forever
  • A draft older than a month has to be re-reviewed rather than quietly going out stale
  • An Instagram post with no image is refused at the point of approval
  • Approval never implies publication — the two are deliberately separate steps
  • Every refusal is recorded, so "Piazza declined to post" is itself auditable
