What Penna Does For You

Penna watches your website's content the way an editor watches a magazine — spotting the page that's quietly bleeding traffic, the article too thin to rank, and the piece that's been sitting untouched for four years while your competitors refreshed theirs.

Then she builds the brief: what to write, which questions to answer, and how to structure it so AI answer engines can quote you.

Everything Penna Can Do6 nightly checks

Finding the problems

  • Raises a critical alert on a page that had real traffic last period and none now — a page you've lost
  • Flags content decay, where a page is still working but clearly sliding
  • Flags thin pages getting impressions without enough substance to rank
  • Flags pages ranking well that nobody clicks — a headline problem, not a content problem
  • Flags pages several years old that are still pulling impressions and deserve a refresh
  • Flags rising pages worth doubling down on while they climb

The content brief

  • Picks opportunities from searches sitting just off page one with real search volume behind them
  • Clusters near-duplicate searches so you write one strong page rather than three competing ones
  • Builds each brief with a primary keyword, supporting keywords, and a suggested headline
  • Includes a fixed editorial spec: an eight-step process for matching what already ranks, seven content requirements, and a fourteen-point on-page checklist
  • Specifies which structured data types the page needs
  • Sets one clear call to action per piece, based on how you actually take enquiries
  • Bans the giveaway AI vocabulary outright

Written to be quoted by AI

  • Requires question-shaped headings with self-contained 40–60 word answers directly underneath — the format answer engines extract
  • Requires the answer first, everywhere, rather than buried under preamble
  • Requires real comparison tables and genuine topical coverage
  • Requires first-hand experience language and at least one original photo or piece of data
  • Insists a refresh changes substance rather than just the date

Into your website

  • Turns an approved brief into a WordPress draft, structured with the question headings and answer placeholders in place
  • Embeds the full brief in the draft so whoever writes it has the spec in front of them
  • Only ever creates a draft — it refuses outright if WordPress returns anything other than draft status
