What Annunci Does For You

Annunci is the most thorough agent on the team. Every night he runs more than sixty separate checks across your Google Ads account — the search terms burning money, the budget stuck on the wrong campaign, the landing page that quietly started erroring — and turns each one into a specific, evidenced recommendation waiting for your yes.

He is deliberately built to catch the boring, expensive things a human only finds during a quarterly audit.

Everything Annunci Can Do60+ nightly checks

Finding wasted spend

  • Flags search terms that took real clicks and real money with zero conversions, and drafts the negative keyword
  • Flags individual keywords burning spend without converting
  • Catches searches that aren't your business at all — wrong intent, wrong service
  • Spots competitor names eating your budget
  • Warns when you're paying for clicks on your own brand name
  • Groups your search terms into themes, so you see the losing theme rather than 400 separate terms — and the winning ones worth expanding

Budget and bidding

  • Finds campaigns that are working but capped by budget — the winners you're throttling
  • Suggests where to move budget between campaigns, with the numbers behind it
  • Flags when you're ready to move up to a smarter bid strategy — and when you don't have enough conversion data to do it safely
  • Tracks how much impression share you're losing to rank, and why
  • Warns when a campaign is still in its learning phase, so you don't judge it too early
  • Warns when the window is too small to draw a conclusion from at all

Leaks in targeting

  • Device leaks — spend going to a device that never converts
  • Geographic leaks, including the setting that shows ads to people merely *interested* in your area rather than in it
  • Time-of-day leaks, and campaigns running outside your business hours
  • Low quality score keywords that are quietly taxing every click

Structural hygiene

  • Duplicate keywords competing against each other
  • Campaigns bidding against your own other campaigns
  • Overlapping geographic targets
  • Missing ad schedules, missing country exclusions, missing negative keyword lists
  • Orphaned conversion actions still sitting in the account
  • Google's own auto-apply recommendations left switched on — changing your account without asking you

After the click

  • Fetches your actual landing pages and flags broken final URLs — ad clicks hitting a dead end
  • Checks the page loads, is real HTML, and isn't painfully slow
  • Flags pages with no conversion tracking tag firing
  • Flags pages with no clear conversion path at all
  • Flags forms long enough to lose people
  • Checks the message on the page actually matches the ad that sent them there

Conversion truth

  • Flags when no primary conversion action is configured — the single most common reason Google optimizes toward nothing
  • Flags Google-hosted conversion actions worth reviewing
  • Holds back conversion-dependent advice when the underlying tracking can't be trusted
  • Produces a Conversion Truth report showing which numbers are safe to act on

Watching Google itself

  • Detects silent changes Google made to your account that nobody approved
  • Flags unexplained changes coming through the API
  • Watches Google's own automation for budget starvation and switched-off guardrails

Growth work

  • Drafts new campaign structures as paused drafts with a hard daily budget cap
  • Suggests A/B experiments worth running
  • Writes ad-copy briefs and headline packets for approval
  • Flags Local Services Ads eligibility, and charged leads you never worked
  • Produces a full account scorecard you can print

Where Annunci Sits In The Swarm

Your agents don't sit in a chat room talking to each other — they share one workspace. Every night, on a fixed schedule, each agent writes what it found onto a shared findings board and into a single approval queue. Every other agent can read that board, and so can you. A few agents also hand each other specific intelligence directly. The result is one queue of decisions instead of fifteen inboxes.
